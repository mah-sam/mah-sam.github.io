An open-source Python application (PyQt6) that operates DIY push-broom hyperspectral imaging (HSI) systems from a single interface. It integrates camera control, stepper-motor synchronization, data acquisition, spectral calibration, and hyperspectral cube assembly, lowering the barrier to entry for researchers building custom HSI hardware.

Published in SoftwareX (2026) as "HSI Control Suite: An Integrated GUI for Operating and Acquiring Data from DIY Push-Broom Hyperspectral Imaging Systems."
