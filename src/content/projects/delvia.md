An AI health tracker for Android and the web, with the iPhone app in testing. People track their health over time through sessions, chat-style updates where they upload lab reports and describe their symptoms, diet, medications and treatments. An AI agent explains the results, records them as structured data and suggests follow-up tests.

Built with Expo React Native, TypeScript and Laravel.
